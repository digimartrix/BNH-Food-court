Frequently Asked Questions

Is your food Halal certified? ▼

Yes! All our food is 100% Halal certified. We follow strict Halal guidelines in sourcing and preparation of all our meat and poultry products.

Do you provide free delivery? ▼

Yes, we offer free delivery within Pichanur, JCT College hostel areas, and nearby locations. Contact us on WhatsApp for fast delivery!

Do you take bulk orders for events and gatherings? ▼

Absolutely! We handle bulk orders for college celebrations, hostel gatherings, family events, and office functions. Please call or WhatsApp us in advance.

Do you serve both Veg and Non-Veg? ▼

Yes! We have dedicated vegetarian and non-vegetarian selections including Veg Biryani, Paneer Biryani, Veg Meals, Crispy Dosa, Fried Rice, Noodles, and more.

What are your opening hours? ▼

We are open every single day from 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM, including weekends and holidays.
